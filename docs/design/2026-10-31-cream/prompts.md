## Final icon

Use case: logo-brand. Create a NEW app icon for 铺记, a friendly small shop inventory app. The attached image is ONLY A REFERENCE FOR THE SIMPLE CUT-PAPER COLLAGE TECHNIQUE: a few large flat geometric pieces assembled into a charming pictogram. DO NOT reuse its colors or objects. Specifically no hearts, stars, sparkles, open boxes, wings, folded ribbon, hands, slate teal or navy background. New subject: a tiny welcoming neighborhood STOREFRONT, assembled from just four or five clean overlapping paper-like shapes: a simple cream storefront body, a muted terracotta awning/roof shape, a small dark olive rectangular doorway, and a pale ochre counter/window rectangle. Make the silhouette clever, compact, graphic and pleasantly asymmetric, like a well-designed Japanese stationery pictogram made of colored paper pieces. No detail inside the window, no text, no inventory bars, no price tag lettering, no thin lines. Color palette independent from the reference and independent from UI: soft pale apricot background #EFDCC7, off-white #FFF8E9, warm burnt clay #B96449, muted olive #626B4D, ochre #CEA56B. Solid large flat color shapes, subtly softened corners, exceptional optical balance, generous negative space. One single square app icon, 1024x1024, simple softly rounded square background filling the canvas. Avoid gradients, 3D, realism, paper texture, shadows, decorative borders, drop shadows, tiny embellishments, multiple icons, presentation board, any letters. Simple shape collage feeling is the ONLY thing borrowed from reference.
